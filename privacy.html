Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains what information gandgco ("we", "us") handles when you visit this site or buy our products.

This website

This site is static and sets no cookies of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log standard technical data (such as IP address and user agent) under its own privacy statement.

Purchases

Orders are processed by the selling platform (for example, Gumroad). The platform collects the information needed to complete your purchase (email, payment details) under its own privacy policy. We receive only what the platform shares with sellers, typically your email address and order details, which we use to deliver your purchase and provide support.

What we never do

We do not sell your information, run advertising trackers on this site, or share your details with third parties beyond the platforms that process your order.
